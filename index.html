Questions people ask

Is Zary free?

Yes. Every feature on this page is free to use, and there are no ads.

Which currencies can I use?

Zary shows amounts in West African CFA francs by default. You can switch to euros, US dollars or British pounds in your profile settings.

How does sign-in work?

After your email and password, Zary asks for a six-digit code. If you step away, it signs you out after a few minutes of inactivity and warns you a minute before.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Export your expenses as a CSV file from the Expenses page and open it in any spreadsheet.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Zary adapts to any screen. On a phone the navigation moves to the bottom of the screen, within reach of your thumb.

Can I look around before creating an account?

Yes. Sign in with demo@zary.app and the password demo123.
